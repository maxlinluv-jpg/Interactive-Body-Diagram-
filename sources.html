EVIDENCE & REFERENCES

Follow the science.

The educational claims in this project are intended to be traceable to authoritative public-health or scientific sources. Sources are used for background, anatomy, nutrient functions, and dietary-pattern guidance.

Evidence policy for this project

Claims are written conservatively. The site avoids treating “traditional” or “modern” as inherently healthy or unhealthy, and it distinguishes established biological functions from broader associations that require more research.

IMPORTANT

Educational, not medical advice.

The model is designed for learning about anatomy, digestion, and nutrition. Health scores and simulations are visualizations created for the project; they are not clinical measurements or predictions about an individual person's health.
