DIGESTION JOURNEY

From a bite
to a nutrient.

Digestion is a sequence, not a single event. The simulator now slows the journey down so an audience can follow the major stages.

01 · MOUTH

Mechanical start

Chewing breaks food into smaller pieces. Saliva moistens food and begins digestion of some carbohydrates.

02 · ESOPHAGUS

Transport

Swallowing moves the food bolus through the esophagus toward the stomach using coordinated muscle contractions.

03 · STOMACH

Mix & break down

Muscular mixing and gastric secretions turn food into a more fluid mixture and begin substantial protein digestion.

04 · SMALL INTESTINE

Absorption

Digestive enzymes and bile help break nutrients down. Much of the absorption of nutrients occurs here.

05 · LARGE INTESTINE

Water & microbiome

Water and electrolytes are reclaimed while microbes interact with remaining material in the colon.

Try it: open the interactive simulator, choose a food, and watch the progress bar and particle move through the model over several seconds.
