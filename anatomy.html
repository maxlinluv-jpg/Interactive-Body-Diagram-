ANATOMY GUIDE

A map of the
human body.

Use the interactive model to explore organs, then come here for a slower explanation of where each structure sits and why it matters.

◉

Brain

The brain coordinates movement, senses, memory, thought, and many automatic processes.

Location · inside the skull
♥

Heart

A muscular pump that moves blood through the lungs and around the rest of the body.

Location · center-left of the chest
◌

Lungs

Paired respiratory organs where oxygen enters the blood and carbon dioxide leaves it.

Location · right and left sides of the chest
◆

Liver

Processes nutrients, produces bile, stores energy, and performs many metabolic functions.

Location · upper-right abdomen
◒

Stomach

Stores and mechanically mixes food while acid and enzymes begin breaking it down.

Location · upper-left abdomen
◎

Intestines

The small and large intestines continue digestion, absorb nutrients and water, and interact with the gut microbiome.

Location · most of the abdomen
