NUTRITION LAB

Food is information
for the body.

Different nutrients participate in different processes. Use these pages to understand the concepts before experimenting in the simulator.

ENERGY

Carbohydrates

Digestible carbohydrates can be broken down into simple sugars and contribute to energy availability. Fiber behaves differently and supports digestive health.

BUILD & REPAIR

Protein

Proteins supply amino acids used to build and maintain tissues and to make enzymes, transport proteins, and many other molecules.

CELL MEMBRANES

Fats

Dietary fats provide energy and fatty acids, help form cell membranes, and help the body absorb fat-soluble vitamins.

DIGESTION

Fiber

Fiber is not digested like most carbohydrates. It can influence stool formation, gut transit, and interactions with intestinal microbes.

PROTECTION

Micronutrients

Vitamins and minerals support many biochemical reactions. They are needed in smaller amounts but remain essential to normal physiology.

SIGNALING

Omega-3 fatty acids

Omega-3 fatty acids are components of cell membranes and participate in signaling pathways involved in normal physiological processes.
