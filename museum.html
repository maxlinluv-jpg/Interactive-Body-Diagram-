Nutrition studio

What food gives the body.

Food supplies energy and the raw materials used for growth, repair, signaling, and everyday function. Different nutrients do different jobs.

◈

Carbohydrates

Major energy source. Digestion breaks many carbohydrates into sugars that cells can use.

ENERGY
✳

Protein

Provides amino acids used to build and repair tissues and make enzymes and other proteins.

BUILD + REPAIR
◌

Fats

Provide concentrated energy, help absorb vitamins A, D, E, and K, and form cell membranes.

STORAGE + STRUCTURE
⌘

Fiber

A type of carbohydrate that isn't fully digested by human enzymes; some fibers feed gut microbes.

GUT FUNCTION
◍

Water

Helps transport substances, regulate temperature, and support chemical reactions.

TRANSPORT
✧

Vitamins

Organic compounds needed in small amounts for many body processes.

REGULATION
⬡

Minerals

Elements such as calcium, iron, and potassium support bones, oxygen transport, and nerve function.

STRUCTURE + SIGNALS
↗

Food patterns

Variety across food groups can help supply the mix of nutrients the body needs.

BALANCE OVER TIME
