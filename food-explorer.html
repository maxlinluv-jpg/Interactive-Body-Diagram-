Explore foods commonly found across dietary patterns and learn what nutrients they provide. A food's place in a dietary pattern does not by itself determine whether the whole pattern is healthy.

HOW TO READ THIS

Food is more than a single nutrient.

Nutrition depends on the overall dietary pattern, amounts consumed, preparation, and individual needs. This explorer focuses on observable characteristics such as fiber, protein, fats, added sugars, sodium, and degree of processing rather than labeling individual foods as simply “good” or “bad.”
